# Submission framing and current title

**Date:** 30 October 2026  
**Submission:** *WELCOME TO THE JUNGLE: AGENT ARENAS, UNLIKELY COLLABORATIONS, AND THE SEARCH FOR SCIENTIFIC IDEAS THAT MATTER*  
**Implemented artifact:** ResearcherConnect v0.3

## Current implementation

The existing prototype assesses pairs of researchers using profiles, an optional scientific opportunity, a deterministic structural heuristic and one of two model-assessment modes: persona conversation with synthesis or repeated binary judge votes. It stores the resulting assessments and human feedback. Development observations are exploratory; no completed formal validation study is claimed.

## Planned development

The proposed agent arena will investigate alternative multi-agent arrangements for exploring connections, developing possible research directions and evaluating their scientific potential with human assessors. Team formation, arena mechanisms and the systematic generation and evaluation of research sketches remain future work. The current conversation prompts continue to recommend whether researchers should meet; their recognition mandate is part of the inspected implementation.
